# M2: Zoning and buildings

M2 puts a city on the roads. The player paints zones on cells along the streets, and buildings appear on them at a fixed rate. It is also where the game gets its look: lighting, shadows, three graphics presets and the first building models. No demand, pops or money yet.

The full context is in [the PRD](../PRD.md), under "M2 Zoning and buildings" and "Graphics presets". The zoom bands that buildings are drawn in are under "Zoom bands".

## Exit test

All of these pass in one Playwright walkthrough, on WebGPU where available and on the WebGL 2 fallback.

1. Open the zone tool. Cells show along both sides of every street, six deep. None lie on water, on steep ground or under another road.
2. Paint R1 along one side of a street. The cells change colour, and the command appears in the activity log with a player id and a tick.
3. Wait. Houses appear along the street over time. Two houses of one type have different colours.
4. Paint each of the five zones at each of the three densities. Each gets buildings of its own kind.
5. Paint a shallow R2 strip and a deep one. The shallow strip gets townhomes and the deep one apartments. An R3 lot smaller than 4 × 4 stays empty, and the overlay says why.
6. Rezone a built R1 lot to C2. The house stays. Clear the house, and a C2 building grows there.
7. Build a street 12 cells from a parallel one and zone both strips C3. One large building stands across both strips.
8. Draw a road through a row of houses. The preview marks the houses in the way and counts them. After the click they are gone.
9. Bulldoze a built street. Its cells and buildings go. Undo. The street and its zoning come back, and buildings grow again.
10. Draw a street beside a zoned strip. Its end snaps to a cell corner.
11. Zoom out. Buildings are full meshes in the street band, plain boxes in the district band and flat blocks coloured by zone in the city band.
12. Switch between Low, Balanced and High. Balanced has shadows near the camera and Low has none. The state hash does not change.
13. Reload the page. The same roads, zoning and buildings come back, in the same colours.
14. A replay of the recorded command log from the same seed produces the same state hash.

## Numbers fixed for M2

| What | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Cell | 8 m square |
| Zoning depth | Six cells from the road's edge, on both sides of every zonable segment |
| Zones | Residential, commercial, office, industrial and mixed |
| Densities | 1, 2 and 3. All three can be painted. The unlock for 2 and 3 arrives in M4 |
| Competing roads | The older segment keeps its cells |
| Lot | A rectangle of same-zone, same-density cells fronting a road, from 1 × 1 to 6 × 6 |
| Through-lot | Up to 12 × 12, for commercial and industrial at density 3 (issue 14) |
| Housing types | The six in the PRD table, with its lot sizes and resident numbers |
| Growth | A fixed number of buildings per game day, from `buildings.json`. Every zone grows |
| Zoning command | One `ZoneCells` per stroke. It names rectangles of ground, not cell ids |
| Graphics presets | Low, Balanced and High. Balanced is the default. No preset changes the sim |
| Theme | One, North American. Models are generator functions in the repo |
| Scale check | The 30,000 segment city from M1 with about 100,000 buildings |

Cell numbers are set in `packages/sim/balance/zoning.json` by issue 01. Building types, lot sizes, resident numbers and the growth rate are set in `packages/sim/balance/buildings.json` by issues 09 and 10. They are starting values to tune.

## Decisions to make inside M2

- **Where cells live.** A cell's zone and density are state, because a player set them. Whether a cell is blocked follows from the roads, the terrain and the segment ages. Issue 03 decides whether that part is saved or derived like the spatial index, and tests that a city saved and reloaded mid-run behaves the same as one that never stopped.
- **What an upgrade does to cells.** A wider road pushes its edge out. The proposal in issue 03 is that cells keep their names and move with the edge, so zoning and buildings stay with the road.
- **Lots along a curve.** Cells on a bend are not a clean grid. Issue 09 decides how lots fit there.
- **Lot sizes for commercial, office, industrial and mixed.** The PRD sets them for housing only. Issue 09 proposes three or four types per density and adds the table to the PRD. Jobs per building stay an open question for M3.
- **Large commercial and industrial lots.** The PRD leaves this for the M2 spec and proposes a through-lot. I took the proposal: issue 14 builds it, for commercial and industrial at density 3, with zoning depth left at six cells. Say so if you meant something else.
- **The growth rate.** The roadmap says a fixed rate and gives no number. Issue 10 picks one so a new street fills in about a game month.
- **What undo brings back.** Issue 16 proposes that undo restores roads and zoning and never buildings. The grower builds again. This matters more in M4, when clearing a building costs money.
- **Where cell snapping sits in the snap order.** Issue 08 decides and records it under "How snapping works" in the PRD.
- **The reference machines for Low and Balanced.** An open question in the PRD. Issue 02 starts with thresholds measured on the CI machine. The answer is needed by the close-out.

## Out of scope

Demand bars, pops, jobs, vacancy and abandonment, land value, money changing hands, the eminent domain price, the milestone that unlocks densities 2 and 3, traffic, overlays other than zoning, services, multiplayer.

Redevelopment of built land after a rezone belongs to M3. It needs land value and demand to say when an owner rebuilds. In M2 a rezoned building stays until the player clears it or its road goes.

Displaced pops belong to M3. In M2 a removed building is gone and nothing moves.

The scale checkpoints at 50,000, 200,000 and 500,000 residents start in M3. M2 measures a city of about 100,000 buildings, because that is where draw calls can first go wrong.

Water reflections and the other High extras are M11. Car counts and street detail per preset are M5. M2 has no day and night cycle.

A European theme is v2. Authored models are not planned.

The two-tab spike is separate work and does not block M2. It is issue #94 and the list under "M1 review" in the PRD. M2 already follows what the review found: `ZoneCells` names ground and `BulldozeBuilding` carries the lot with the id, so neither acts on a stale id.

## One risk to settle early

About 100,000 buildings at full size is the first item under "Where the time goes" in the PRD. Shadows on instanced meshes are hard to add late, which is why the PRD puts the presets at the start of M2. So issue 02 goes first on the client side and runs beside the sim work, and issue 13 measures the full count before the close-out.

On the sim side the risk is the cell count. Thirty thousand segments carry on the order of a million cells. Issue 03 measures generation and upkeep on the scale city before anything is built on top.

### What can run in parallel

The numbering is one safe order, not the only one. Each GitHub issue lists what blocks it and what it blocks. An issue can start as soon as everything that blocks it is merged.

| Step | Ready to start | Once this is merged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 01 and 02 | M1 |
| 2 | 03 | 01 |
| 3 | 04 | 03 |
| 4 | 05 and 09 | 04 |
| 5 | 06, 08 and 10 | 05 for 06 and 08. 09 for 10 |
| 6 | 07, 11 and 12 | 06 for 07. 10 for 11. 02 and 10 for 12 |
| 7 | 13, 14, 15 and 16 | 12 for 13. 11 for 14 and 16. 07, 11 and 12 for 15 |
| 8 | 17 | everything |

There are two chains after issue 04. The zoning chain is 05, 06 and 07, with snapping in 08 hanging off 05. The building chain is 09, 10 and 11, with through-lots in 14 and undo in 16 at its end. Issue 02 touches no sim code and runs beside all of it. Building rendering, 12 and 13, needs both 02 and the grower. Issue 15 is where the chains meet.

Two things to watch when working in parallel. Issues 01, 03 and 09 each bump `SNAPSHOT_VERSION`, and 16 does if the undo record changes shape. They are in one chain, so they merge in order, but a branch started early has to take the next number when it rebases. Issues 05 and 09 both raise the protocol version. Issues 01, 03, 04, 10 and 11 all change the replay fixtures, so expect to refresh fixture hashes after a rebase and say why in the pull request.
